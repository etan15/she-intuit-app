SHE Accountant

Private internal tool of Southern Home Estates Inc.

SHE Accountant is an internal application used only by Southern Home Estates Inc. to read its own QuickBooks Online accounting data for bookkeeping and reporting. It is not offered to the public and cannot be installed by other companies.

Connection status

If you arrived here after connecting or disconnecting QuickBooks, the action is complete. You can close this page. To disconnect at any time, remove "SHE Accountant" under Settings → Apps in QuickBooks Online.
