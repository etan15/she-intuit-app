Privacy Policy

SHE Accountant · Effective November 8, 2026

What data is accessed

With the Company's authorization, SHE Accountant reads accounting data from Southern Home Estates Inc.'s own QuickBooks Online files: transactions, accounts, vendors, customers, classes, reports and attachment details. Access is read-only and limited to the QuickBooks accounting scope.

How it is used

The data is used solely for the Company's internal bookkeeping, reconciliation and reporting.

Where it is stored

Data and access tokens are stored only on computers owned and controlled by the Company, on encrypted storage, readable only by authorized Company users. Data is processed by software running on those computers.

Sharing

The data is not sold, rented or shared with third parties, and is not used for advertising. It may be provided to the Company's own accountants or tax advisors at the Company's direction.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept as long as the Company needs it for its records and is deleted from Company systems when no longer required. Disconnecting the app in QuickBooks Online stops all further access.
